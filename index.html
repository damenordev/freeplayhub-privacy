Privacy Policy — FreePlay Hub

Last updated: October 20, 2026

FreePlay Hub is an independent catalog of free-to-play PC and browser games. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Accounts, advertising and analytics

The app has no sign-up or user accounts, advertising, in-app purchases, analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not operate an app backend or receive your favorites or browsing history.

Local storage and Android backup

Favorite game IDs and the onboarding-completed setting are stored in the app's private storage on your device. Android may back up and restore app data according to your device and account backup settings; FreePlay Hub does not operate that backup service.

Network requests and third parties

The app uses HTTPS to request game information and artwork from FreeToGame and its content servers. These requests expose your IP address and ordinary connection metadata to those providers. FreeToGame's published policy describes server logs, including IP addresses and request timestamps; its own privacy practices apply. See https://www.freetogame.com/privacy-policy. We do not add advertising identifiers, account details or favorites to these requests.

External game websites

The Play button opens a game's website in your external browser. These websites and games are operated by their respective publishers and have their own privacy policies. FreePlay Hub does not control their data practices or your browser's cookies.

Retention and deletion

Favorites remain locally until you remove them, clear the app's storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Android system backups may be retained or restored according to the backup service's settings; manage those copies through your Android account. Third-party network logs are governed by the relevant provider's policy. There is no FreePlay Hub account or server-side profile to delete.

Contact and changes

For privacy questions, contact damenor.develop@gmail.com. If you email us, your email provider delivers the address and information you choose to include; please do not send unnecessary sensitive information. Changes to this policy will be reflected in its updated date and in future app releases.
